Site Name: HikeZim

Domain: hikezim.org

Logo: Mountain silhouette of Eastern Highlands with trail path

I chose HikeZim because it is short, memorable, and clearly describes the site focus - hiking in Zimbabwe. It is brandable for both local and international hikers.

Site Purpose

The purpose of HikeZim is to be a central hub for hiking information in Zimbabwe. Zimbabwe has awesome hiking in places like Eastern Highlands, Matopo Hills, and Nyanga, but information is scattered online.

  • Top hiking spots with photos and detailed descriptions
  • Trail maps, difficulty levels, distance, and elevation
  • Tips for hiking in Zimbabwe: packing, seasons, safety, fees
  • Contact form for guided hikes

Scenarios

  • What is the easiest trail in Matopo Hills for a beginner who has only 3 hours?
  • How difficult is Mount Nyangani hike and what equipment do I need for it?
  • Where can I find a local guide and what are the park fees for Nyanga National Park?
